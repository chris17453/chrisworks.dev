How it was validated

Three gates. Each bar calibrated from measurement, never assumed.

GateWhat it catches
Face similarity (InsightFace)A completely different person. Collapses under stylisation, so it cannot judge stylised identity.
Appearance (CLIP, face crop)Wrong beard, hair, build, age — everything the face metric ignores. The real discriminator.
Style probe (CLIP text)An unchanged photograph masquerading as a stylised avatar.

The adversarial suite

Twelve prompts built to break identity: profile, three-quarter, low angle, four styles, outdoor, stage, shouting, laughing.

What failed, with numbers

Ten approaches measured and rejected. Kept because each one explains a decision.

v2: descriptive captions

Ask for a profile and it returns a woman. Describing his beard in captions meant the words owned the face, not the trigger.

v4: five variables changed at once

Total collapse. A cartoon vegetable, an empty room, a cartoon child.
